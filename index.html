Rule 01
10 miles
From center
Orders that qualify for free local delivery must be within 10 miles of the center from which the item is purchased.
Rule 02
$500
Minimum
Orders eligible for free delivery must have a minimum order value of $500. Orders below this threshold do not qualify for free local delivery.
Rule 03
Delivery rates
$2 / mile
Min. $45
Delivery is charged at $2 per mile with a minimum of $45. Alternatively, shipping is available at published UPS rates — not our rates — plus a packing fee.
Rule 04
Delivery time
2–3 business
Days
Free and paid deliveries will occur within two to three business days after the order is completed. The salesperson is required to request a delivery date at the time of quality control.

If a salesperson does not request delivery at that time, the delivery time clock starts when you request the date — not when the order is completed. This will put you in a bad position with your client.
Rule 05
Plan ahead
& communicate
Outside salespeople may — and should — consider delivering their orders when possible. It's a great opportunity to have face time with your client, check in, and get more sales.
Important exception
There are exceptions to the rule. We just need to understand this and not be delivering $200 orders that take three hours to deliver. Amazon — yes, exceptions to some point — and other clients as well.
Remember: Plan ahead, communicate, and deliver value
Great delivery service builds great relationships… and keeps clients coming back.

04

30
sec
Rule
What is the 30 second rule?
Every time a task is completed, take 30 seconds to clean up your area before moving on.
Putting tools away, throwing out scrap, organizing materials, and resetting the workspace keeps the production floor cleaner, safer, and more efficient throughout the day.
When everyone contributes small cleanup moments consistently, closing down at the end of the day becomes dramatically faster and easier for the entire team.
Benefits of the 30 second rule
Prevents clutter from building up
Improves workflow and efficiency
Creates a safer production environment
Protects equipment and materials
Makes end-of-day cleanup easier for everyone
Reinforces professionalism and teamwork
Team message
Small cleanup habits create big results.
30 seconds now saves hours later.

Process policy

Site survey

The right process. Better results.

CommunicationOwnershipAccountability

Last updated: June 2025

Guideline 01
Verify the need first
When speaking with customers about their project, please ensure that conducting a site survey is the correct step.
Guideline 02
The phone call can solve it
We have had instances where we received a request, and when the outside sales rep contacted the customer, they resolved the issue without needing an on-site visit.
Guideline 03
Ask all the right questions
We must be more diligent and ask all the right questions. Please don't take the shortcut and order a site survey without proper qualification first.
Guideline 04
Questions? Reach out.
If you have any questions, please reach out so we can discuss them further. Open communication is always the right path.
Communication matters
Management will assign an outside sales rep (OSR) to complete the site survey. If the OSR does not respond and the prospect or client calls the center back, please let management know.
Tell clients to allow 24–48 hrs for a response for scheduling. In a rush situation, always submit your form and inform management of the urgency.
Timely survey requests and asking the right questions can strengthen our communication and the communication with our clients.
Remember
Ask first. Verify need. Add value.
The right conversation today can save time for everyone.
